Hold Right ⌥ (Option) anywhere on your Mac → speak → text appears.
No internet. No subscription. No account. Completely free.

Works in: Messages, Gmail, Slack, Notion, Word, Notes — anywhere
you can type.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  REQUIREMENTS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, or M4 chip)
  • macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • ~2 GB free disk space (for the AI model, one-time download)
  • Internet connection (only during installation)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  INSTALLATION  (takes 5–15 minutes)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Move the VoiceType folder somewhere permanent
     (Documents, home folder — anywhere you like).
     Don't move it after installing.

  2. Double-click "Install VoiceType.command"
     → macOS may ask "Are you sure you want to open this?" → click Open
     → A Terminal window opens and walks you through everything

  3. Grant two permissions when prompted:
     • Accessibility — for the hotkey to work
     • Microphone    — to record your voice (asked automatically)

  That's it!

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HOW TO USE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Click the mic icon 🎙️ in your menu bar (top-right of screen)
  2. Click "Turn ON"  (icon turns blue)
  3. Click inside any text field
  4. Hold Right ⌥ (the Option key on the RIGHT side of spacebar)
  5. Speak
  6. Release ⌥ — text pastes automatically!

  The icon colours tell you what's happening:
    ⚫ Dimmed  = Off or paused
    🟡 Yellow  = Loading (wait a few seconds)
    🔵 Normal  = Ready — hold Right ⌥ to speak
    🔴 Red     = Recording your voice
    🟠 Orange  = Transcribing

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DASHBOARD
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Click the menu bar icon → Open Dashboard to access:

  • Home      — status, stats, time-saved counter, today's history
  • History   — all past transcriptions by date
  • Grammar   — paste any text and fix grammar / change tone
  • Settings  — language, AI model, auto-cleanup, grammar fix

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  VOICE COMMANDS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  While recording, say these exactly to trigger actions:

    "new line"         → press Return
    "new paragraph"    → press Return twice
    "period"           → insert .
    "comma"            → insert ,
    "question mark"    → insert ?
    "exclamation mark" → insert !
    "clear that"       → undo last paste
    "all caps"         → next dictation will be ALL CAPS

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  TROUBLESHOOTING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Nothing appears in menu bar?
  → Double-click "Install VoiceType.command" again to fix auto-start

  Stays yellow / won't turn on?
  → Wait up to 20 seconds on first launch (model is loading)
  → Check: Terminal → cat /tmp/vt_worker_stderr.log

  Hotkey not recording?
  → Make sure Accessibility permission is ON for VoiceType
  → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility

  Text pastes in wrong place?
  → Click inside a text field BEFORE holding Right ⌥

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  UNINSTALL
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Double-click "Uninstall VoiceType.command"
  Then drag the VoiceType folder to the Trash.
